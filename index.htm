History

Our journey of service and stewardship

From a small collective of families to a respected institution, our path has been shaped by compassion, discipline and continuity.

Formation

The Mahajan was founded with a vision of bringing together families under one ethical and cultural umbrella.

Growth

With steady participation and shared leadership, the organization expanded into education, welfare and community programs.

Welfare Initiatives

Major support efforts were introduced for scholarships, medical assistance and emergency relief for those in need.

Community Achievements

Our collective work has helped strengthen local identity, youth participation and broader public goodwill.

Future Vision
